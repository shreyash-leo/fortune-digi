Our Marketing & Media Services

Ready to take your Nashik brand to the next level? At Fortech Media and Marketing, we blend creativity and strategy to deliver marketing and media services that truly drive results — from growing your online presence to telling your brand's story through powerful visuals.

Social Media Marketing

Engage and grow your audience on platforms like Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. We create tailored social media campaigns, content calendars, and community-building strategies that boost brand awareness and drive real engagement.

YouTube Marketing & Video Production

Stand out with professional promotional videos, explainer animations, customer testimonials, and branded YouTube channels. Our videos help capture attention, increase watch time, and convert viewers into loyal customers.

Graphic Design & Creative Services

Capture attention with custom graphics for social media, digital ads, events, and print materials. Our creative designs are crafted to enhance your brand voice and make your message unforgettable.

Branding & Identity Development

Create a lasting impression with a memorable brand identity. From logo design to comprehensive brand guidelines, we craft unique visuals and messaging that reflect your business values and connect with your audience.

Print & Digital Collateral

Support your marketing with high-quality brochures, menus, certificates, and other print or digital materials designed to reinforce your brand and enhance customer experience.

Website Development

Set your business apart with a modern, responsive website built for performance, usability, and search engine rankings. From custom business sites to landing pages and e-commerce solutions, we ensure your online presence is as strong as your brand.

Google My Business Optimisation

Increase your local visibility with a fully optimised Google My Business profile. We ensure your business shows up in "near me" searches, attract positive reviews, and connect seamlessly with local customers online.

Performance Marketing (Google Ads & Meta Ads)

Get measurable results with targeted Google Ads and Meta (Facebook & Instagram) advertising. We design data-driven ad campaigns focused on lead generation, increasing sales, and maximising your return on investment.

Campaign Strategy & Execution

Launch powerful marketing campaigns tailored to your goals, whether it's awareness, product launch, or customer engagement. Our integrated approach combines creativity with analytics for maximum impact.

SEO Services

On-page, technical, and local SEO to help Nashik businesses rank higher on Google and get found by customers actively searching for their products or services.

Content Marketing & Copywriting

Blog content, website copy, and email newsletters that build authority, improve SEO, and keep your audience engaged.

WhatsApp Business Marketing & Automation

Set up and manage WhatsApp Business tools, automated responses, and broadcast campaigns — one of India's fastest-growing customer channels.
